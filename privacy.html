Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This app is a personal video clipping and publishing tool. It lets the account owner edit short videos and publish them to their own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Data we collect

  • TikTok account data: when you log in with TikTok, we receive your basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) and an access token, with your permission.
  • Content you upload: videos, captions and schedule settings you create in the app.

2. How we use it

  • To publish videos you choose to your own TikTok account.
  • To show which account is connected and the status of your posts.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored locally on the device running the app. Access tokens are kept only to perform actions you request and are never shared.

4. Third-party services

The app connects to TikTok to log you in and publish videos. TikTok handles that data under its own Privacy Policy.

5. Data retention

We keep your data only while your account is connected. When you disconnect or delete content, it is removed from the app.

6. Your choices

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time inside the app, or revoke access in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored token. You may also delete your videos and data from the app at any time.

7. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country).

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

9. Contact
