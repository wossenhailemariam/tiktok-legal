Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the operator of this application ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the application and its related features (the "App"). We are committed to handling your information responsibly, collecting only what is necessary to provide the App, and being transparent about our practices. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information required to operate the App and carry out the actions you request.

1.1 Information received from TikTok

When you choose to connect your TikTok account through TikTok's official authorization process, and only with your explicit consent, we receive the following from TikTok:

  • your TikTok open ID, a unique identifier assigned to your account for the App;
  • basic profile information, such as your display name and profile image;
  • an access token and refresh token that allow the App to perform the specific actions you have authorized, such as uploading and publishing videos; and
  • status information about posts you submit through the App, such as whether a publish request succeeded.

We do not receive or store your TikTok password. We do not access your direct messages, followers list, contacts or any content you have not chosen to publish through the App.

1.2 Content you provide

We process the videos, captions, hashtags, schedule settings and other materials you upload, create or edit within the App, solely to enable you to prepare and publish that content.

1.3 Technical information

The App may generate basic technical logs, such as error messages and request timestamps, which are used only to keep the App working correctly and to diagnose problems. We do not use cookies or tracking technologies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above exclusively for the following purposes:

  • to authenticate your TikTok account and display which account is connected;
  • to upload and publish content to your TikTok account when, and only when, you instruct the App to do so;
  • to show you the status and history of your scheduled and published posts;
  • to maintain, secure, troubleshoot and improve the App; and
  • to comply with legal obligations and to enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not use your content to train machine-learning models.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With TikTok, to the extent necessary to publish content you have chosen to post. TikTok's handling of that information is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • With service providers that host or support the App, who may process information only on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations, and only as needed to provide their services.
  • For legal reasons, where disclosure is required by law, regulation, legal process or an enforceable governmental request, or is necessary to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or ourselves.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, in which case the information will remain subject to protections at least as strong as those in this policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. Access tokens are stored securely, are used only to perform the actions you request, and are never exposed to other users. All communication with TikTok takes place over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. While we take these measures seriously, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as your TikTok account remains connected to the App, or as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy. When you disconnect your TikTok account, we delete the associated access and refresh tokens. Videos and other content you delete within the App are removed from our storage. We may retain limited information where required by law or to resolve disputes, and only for as long as that requirement lasts.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, and to request a copy of it in a portable format. You can exercise these rights at any time by contacting us at the address below. In addition, you can:

  • disconnect your TikTok account from within the App, which deletes the stored tokens;
  • revoke the App's access directly in TikTok, under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; and
  • delete any content you have uploaded to the App.

We will respond to verified requests within the time required by applicable law, and in any case within thirty (30) days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13, or under the minimum age required by TikTok or by the law of your country. If we learn that we have collected information from a child in violation of this policy, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one in which you live, including the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those of your jurisdiction. Where required, we take appropriate steps to ensure that your information receives an adequate level of protection consistent with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, the App or applicable law. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page, and where changes are material we will make reasonable efforts to notify you. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our handling of your information, please contact us at info@ameriix.com.
